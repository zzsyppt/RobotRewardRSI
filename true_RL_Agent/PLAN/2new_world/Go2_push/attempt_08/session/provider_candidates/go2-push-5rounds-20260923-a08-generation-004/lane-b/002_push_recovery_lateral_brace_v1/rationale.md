Closest prior program: push_recovery_nonsaturating_stance_v1, the accepted current best (public_dev mean 0.8802). Its dominant failure is fall_dominated, with 15 fall_side versus 1 fall_forward and 3 height_collapse, so sideways toppling is the residual bottleneck. Prior attempts push_recovery_tilt_aware_stance_v2 (tilt-height coupling) and push_recovery_vel_damped_stance_v1 (displacement-gated speed damping) were both formally rejected, so those routes are negative evidence and are not repeated. Sole mechanism change here: a new RewardTerm lateral_brace is added; upright_recovery, stationary_v3, joint_pose, action_rate, fall, all weights, signs, clips, and the empty schedule and diagnostic lists are preserved exactly. The anchor penalizes roll and pitch only through the symmetric squared tilt sum, which is dominated by whichever axis is larger and gives no dedicated lateral signal; a base that is tipping sideways can therefore accumulate roll and lateral drift at little extra cost. lateral_brace returns -(clamp(|roll| - 0.10, 0) + 0.5 * |v_y_scaled|), a one-sided lateral potential that is exactly zero inside a 0.10 rad roll deadband and grows linearly with sideways tilt and lateral base speed. It is asymmetric by construction: it never penalizes forward/backward motion or pitch, so it cannot suppress the sagittal recovery transient that the anchor already handles. The clip [-2, 0] with weight -1.0 bounds the weighted contribution, and the deadband keeps the nominal stance value identical to the anchor. Success definition, push schedule, and all runner-owned surfaces are untouched.
