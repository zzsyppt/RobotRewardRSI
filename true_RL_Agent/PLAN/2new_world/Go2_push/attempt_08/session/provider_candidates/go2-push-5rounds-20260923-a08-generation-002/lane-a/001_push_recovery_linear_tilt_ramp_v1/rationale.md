Closest prior program: push_recovery_nonsaturating_stance_v1, the accepted current-best anchor. Sole mechanism change: the tilt branch of the stance potential callable, renamed upright_recovery_linear_tilt; every other callable body, weight, sign, clip, and the empty schedule and diagnostic lists are preserved. The anchor scored tilt with u = clamp(roll*roll + pitch*pitch - 0.01, 0) and penalty 2*u + 2*u*u, so its tilt pull-back slope is 4*theta*(1 + 2*u) per radian: about 0.4 at the deadband edge and about 0.85 at 0.2 radians. That leaves the first 6 to 15 degrees of a lean almost free, exactly the window in which a lateral perturbation has to be arrested. The anchor's residual public-dev failures are dominated by sideways falls (15 fall_side against 1 fall_forward), consistent with a tilt branch that stays shallow until the lean has already run away, after which only the fall penalty remains. The replacement keeps the identical kernel shape and coefficients (2*x + 2*x*x) but applies them to the tilt-magnitude excess v = clamp(sqrt(roll*roll + pitch*pitch + 1e-6) - 0.1, 0) instead of to the squared-tilt excess u, which makes the tilt branch linear-dominant in the lean angle and mirrors the height branch the previous round already made linear-dominant for the same reason. The deadband is unchanged in value, since 0.1 radians is exactly the anchor's sqrt(0.01) tilt wall, and at 0.9 radians the two forms take the same value again, so the near-goal potential and the deep-lean magnitude are both preserved while the 0.1 to 0.9 radian ramp is strictly steeper. Concretely the slope becomes 2.0 + 4*v per radian, that is 2.0 versus 0.4 at the deadband edge and 2.4 versus 0.85 at 0.2 radians, and a 10 to 25 degree lean now costs about 0.5 to 2.7 weighted stance reward per step instead of about 0.13 to 1.3. The term weight, clip bounds, and the posture, action-rate, velocity-damping and fall terms are untouched, so the low and medium force strata that already recover keep their existing signal while the policy receives a graded early pull-back gradient for the leans that dominate the remaining failures.
