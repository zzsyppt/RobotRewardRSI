Closest prior program: push_recovery_nonsaturating_stance_v1, the accepted current best (public_dev mean 0.8802, dominant failure fall_dominated with 15 fall_side and 1 fall_forward). Two sibling attempts are negative evidence and are not repeated: push_recovery_tilt_aware_stance_v2 added a 4*e*u height-tilt coupling to the same kernel and was formally rejected at 0.5521, and push_recovery_vel_damped_stance_v1 added a displacement-gated velocity penalty and was formally rejected at 0.7656 with fall_side still dominant (32 side falls). Both failed because they perturbed the recovery transient or the near-goal signal rather than the lateral posture itself. Sole mechanism change here: a new RewardTerm lateral_brace is added; the upright_recovery kernel, stationary_v3, joint_pose, action_rate, fall, all their weights, signs, clips, and the empty schedule and diagnostic lists are preserved exactly. The anchor's tilt branch is symmetric in roll and pitch and is already near its deadband at the nominal stance, so a base that has been pushed sideways can hold a large roll at only a modest extra cost while the height branch dominates the gradient; the residual side falls are consistent with that weak lateral slope. lateral_brace returns -g * clamp(roll^2 - 0.01, 0, inf) with g = clamp((e + u) / (e + u + 0.25), 0, 1), where e and u are the same deadbanded height and tilt excesses used by the stance kernel. The gate is near zero at the nominal stance and approaches one when the base is far from it, so the term is inactive when the robot is already standing and grows with displacement, adding lateral restoring slope exactly in the regime where the anchor's symmetric tilt shaping is weakest. Because the gate is smooth and bounded and the term is quadratic in roll, it never blocks the fast recovery transient: at small displacement the penalty is a small fraction of roll^2, and the clip [-2, 0] keeps the weighted contribution bounded at weight -1.0. Unlike the rejected velocity-damped sibling, this term shapes posture rather than speed, so it does not fight the recovery transient. The success definition, push schedule, and all runner-owned surfaces are untouched.
