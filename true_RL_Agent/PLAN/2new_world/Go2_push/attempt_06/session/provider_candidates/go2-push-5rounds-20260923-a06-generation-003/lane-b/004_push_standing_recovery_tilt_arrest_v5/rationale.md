Anchor and evidence. Authoring anchor: accepted current-best push_standing_recovery_height_barrier_v1 (public_dev mean 0.78125). Its one-sided, downward-modulated height barrier addressed the height_collapse-dominated failure reported for the closest prior evaluated candidate push_recovery_height_hinge_v4 (public_dev mean 0.703125; per-192 counts 29 height_collapse, 14 fall_backward, 9 fall_side; high push stratum 18/48, repeated stratum 27/48). The residual failure mass is therefore expected to sit in tilt-dominated fall modes that the exponential stance channel can no longer suppress once it saturates. Closest prior candidate: push_standing_recovery_tilt_arrest_v4 (rejected by the historical-source gate as an exact program repeat; its mechanism was a velocity-gated tilt channel 1.5 * max(tilt - 0.25, 0) * clamp(roll * roll_rate + pitch * pitch_rate, min=0)). That gate outcome is evidence that the v4 mechanism, as written, was not a new experiment relative to the evaluated program set, so the mechanism must be changed rather than renamed. Exact code-level difference from v4: exactly one RewardTerm callable is replaced, upright_tilt_arrest becomes upright_tilt_arrest_v5, and the new mechanism is a tilt-overshoot barrier that penalizes the excess tilt magnitude itself with a steeper-than-quadratic growth, replacing the v4 rate-coupling term. The nominal stance reward exp(-4*(roll^2 + pitch^2) - 4*(base_height_relative - 1)^2), the full height barrier 4*max(0.8 - base_height_relative, 0)^2*(1 + min(max(-v_z, 0), 2)) with v_z = base_lin_vel_scaled[2], and the one-sided tilt barrier 2*max(tilt - 0.25, 0) + max(tilt - 0.25, 0)^2 are preserved unchanged. The new callable adds exactly one mechanism: a cubic overshoot channel tilt_overshoot = max(tilt - 0.25, 0)^3, so the added term is 6.0 * tilt_overshoot. This term is exactly zero below the tilt band, so nominal stance and ordinary recovery-step transients are untouched; above the band it grows faster than the existing quadratic barrier, so the marginal cost of continuing to lean outward rises sharply and the policy receives a usable pre-terminal gradient to arrest tilt growth and convert high-stratum backward and side falls into recovered episodes. The replaced callable deliberately does not include planar or angular velocity damping (that stays in the unchanged stationary_v3 callable), so the new mechanism is a barrier-shaped pre-fall risk channel rather than a fused stationary-recovery composite. Reward shaping cannot change pulse verification, timing, or the success definition, and this candidate changes none of them.
