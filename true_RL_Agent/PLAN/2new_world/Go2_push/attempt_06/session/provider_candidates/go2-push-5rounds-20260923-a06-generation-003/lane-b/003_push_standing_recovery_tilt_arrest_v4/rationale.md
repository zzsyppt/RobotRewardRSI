Anchor and evidence. Authoring anchor: accepted current-best push_standing_recovery_height_barrier_v1 (public_dev mean 0.78125). Its one-sided, downward-modulated height barrier addressed the height_collapse-dominated failure reported for the closest prior evaluated candidate push_recovery_height_hinge_v4 (public_dev mean 0.703125; per-192 counts 29 height_collapse, 14 fall_backward, 9 fall_side; high push stratum 18/48, repeated stratum 27/48). The residual failure mass is therefore expected to sit in tilt-dominated fall modes that the exponential stance channel can no longer suppress once it saturates. Closest prior candidate: push_standing_recovery_tilt_arrest_v3 (public_dev mean not accepted; rejected as a historical exact-program repeat). v3 added a one-sided non-saturating tilt barrier tilt_barrier = 2*max(tilt - 0.25, 0) + max(tilt - 0.25, 0)^2 on top of the anchor stance and height barrier, but it was evaluated as an exact program and produced no accepted improvement, so the barrier alone is insufficient. Exact code-level difference from v3: exactly one RewardTerm callable is replaced, upright_tilt_arrest becomes upright_tilt_arrest_v4, and the new mechanism is a velocity-gated tilt channel. The nominal stance reward exp(-4*(roll^2 + pitch^2) - 4*(base_height_relative - 1)^2), the full height barrier 4*max(0.8 - base_height_relative, 0)^2*(1 + min(max(-v_z, 0), 2)) with v_z = base_lin_vel_scaled[2], and the one-sided tilt barrier 2*max(tilt - 0.25, 0) + max(tilt - 0.25, 0)^2 are preserved unchanged. The new callable adds exactly one mechanism: a tilt-velocity coupling term tilt_vel = (roll * roll_rate + pitch * pitch_rate) where roll_rate = base_ang_vel_scaled[:, 0] and pitch_rate = base_ang_vel_scaled[:, 1], gated by the same excess band excess = max(tilt - 0.25, 0), so the added term is 1.5 * excess * tilt_vel.clamp(min=0.0). This term is exactly zero below the tilt band, so nominal stance and ordinary recovery-step transients are untouched; above the band it contributes a gradient proportional to the outward tilt rate, giving policy updates a usable pre-terminal signal to arrest tilt growth and convert high-stratum backward and side falls into recovered episodes. The replaced callable deliberately does not include planar or angular velocity damping (that stays in the unchanged stationary_v3 callable), so the new mechanism is a barrier-shaped pre-fall risk channel rather than a fused stationary-recovery composite. Reward shaping cannot change pulse verification, timing, or the success definition, and this candidate changes none of them.
