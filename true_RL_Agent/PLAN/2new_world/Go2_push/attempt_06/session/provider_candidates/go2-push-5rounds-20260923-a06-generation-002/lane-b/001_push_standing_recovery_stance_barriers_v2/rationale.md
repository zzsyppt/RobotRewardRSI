Anchor and evidence. Authoring anchor: accepted current-best push_standing_recovery_height_barrier_v1 (public_dev mean 0.78125). Its one-sided, downward-modulated height barrier addressed the height_collapse-dominated failure reported for the closest prior evaluated candidate push_recovery_height_hinge_v4 (public_dev mean 0.703125; per-192 counts 29 height_collapse, 14 fall_backward, 9 fall_side; high push stratum 18/48, repeated stratum 27/48). The residual failure mass is therefore expected to sit in tilt-dominated fall modes that the exponential stance channel can no longer suppress once it saturates. Exact code-level difference from the anchor: exactly one RewardTerm callable is replaced, upright_height_barrier becomes upright_stance_barriers. The nominal stance reward exp(-4*(roll^2 + pitch^2) - 4*(base_height_relative - 1)^2) and the full height barrier 4*max(0.8 - base_height_relative, 0)^2*(1 + min(max(-v_z, 0), 2)) with v_z = base_lin_vel_scaled[2] are preserved unchanged, so nominal standing, mild crouch, and downward-motion risk are shaped exactly as in the anchor. The new callable adds exactly one mechanism: a one-sided, non-saturating tilt barrier with tilt = roll^2 + pitch^2, excess = max(tilt - 0.25, 0) and tilt_barrier = 2*excess + excess^2, and the stance term output is bounded below by clip_min = -3.0. Every other callable, weight, diagnostic and schedule is identical to the anchor. Mechanism hypothesis: below about 0.5 rad combined tilt the barrier is exactly zero, so nominal stance and ordinary recovery-step transients are untouched; above that band it contributes a persistent linear-plus-quadratic gradient precisely where exp(-4*tilt) has decayed to a near-zero slope and the -20 fall term fires only at termination, giving policy updates a usable pre-terminal signal to arrest tilt growth and convert high-stratum backward and side falls into recovered episodes. The replaced callable deliberately does not include planar or angular velocity damping (that stays in the unchanged stationary_v3 callable), so the new mechanism is a barrier-shaped pre-fall risk channel rather than a fused stationary-recovery composite. Reward shaping cannot change pulse verification, timing, or the success definition, and this candidate changes none of them.
